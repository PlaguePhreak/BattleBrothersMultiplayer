# Battle Brothers Multiplayer (BB Co-op)

A mod that aims to let two players share one Battle Brothers world map, each running their own company, and fight together as allies.

Battle Brothers has no networking, but the game's UI browser can make HTTP requests. This mod uses that to talk to a small **relay server** that both players connect to.

**Status: v0.33.0: attack animations for joiners, joiners get the host's campaign settings, 20-second join window before shared battles, banners on the map, turn marker in shared battles, steadier battle copy for joiners, joiners with the host's origin get their own brothers; End round no longer skips other players' brothers, the host's pause pauses everyone; host's round in shared battles, contract panel for helpers, real battlefield for joiners, one campaign and clock, caravan escorts.** Each player sees the other's company on the world map, can join the other's battle as reinforcements, and plays their own brothers in it. Target is the vanilla game first; Legends and other mods come later.

## What it does

- **Attack animations for joiners (v0.33.0):** a joiner's copy of the battle now replays every skill the host's game uses: the skill's sound, arrows and other shots flying for ranged skills, the attacker lunging and the target flinching, one after another in order. Hitpoints and deaths still come from the host. Same protocol (6).
- **Host's campaign settings (v0.32.0):** joiners get all of the host's campaign settings (from the campaign thread).
- **Joiner input fix (v0.31.1):** in a copy of someone else's battle, a unit the copy couldn't put in place (shown as "Copy differs from the host" in the log) kept the map locked for the whole turn, so the joiner couldn't do anything. Now only your own acting brother's move holds the input. Same protocol (6).
- **Join window (v0.31.0):** when a battle starts and other players are online, it waits up to 20 seconds before round 1. Nearby players get the join prompt with the countdown; whoever joins in time has their brothers placed right next to their side (not at the map edge) and plays them from round 1. The battle starts as soon as everyone has joined or said no. Players out of range or busy say no by themselves. Joining later still works and brings brothers in at the edge as before. All co-op players need v0.31.0 (protocol 6).
- **Banners on the map (v0.29.0):** other players' companies carry their own banner and keep their "X's company" name, like a player's company. A clanmate's company carries your banner.
- **Turn marker (v0.28.0):** in a shared battle, the brother whose turn it is (anyone's) has the game's red arrow hopping over his head, on the host's screen and in every joiner's copy, so everyone can see who is acting. Also fixed: when one of the host's brothers died, the joiner's copy kept him in its turn bar, then locked up (and could crash when a skill was selected); the copy now takes dead units off its turn bar.
- **Steadier battle copy (v0.27.0):** a joiner's copy of the battle only moves its turn bar for the joiner's own brothers. It used to chase every unit's turn in its own turn order, which ran ahead of the host and jumped back on the joiner's turn. The copy also no longer ends the joiner's turn by itself while its turn bar catches up: only the joiner's End turn (button, key or action strip) does.
- **Same origin, different brothers (v0.26.0):** a joiner who picks the same origin as the host used to get the host's exact starting brothers (the map seed also rolled the company). A joiner's company is now rolled from the map seed plus their player name, so everyone's brothers differ; the map still matches.
- **End round leaves friends' turns alone (v0.25.0):** when the host pressed End round (the button or Enter), the game also skipped the brothers other players control in that battle. Now only the host's own brothers are skipped. A joiner pressing End round in their copy just ends their brother's turn. Same protocol (5).
- **Host's round number (v0.23.0):** a joiner's copy of the battle shows the host's round in the top bar, not its own count (its turn bar runs extra rounds while catching up). Same protocol as v0.22.0 (5).
- **The battlefield is the control field (v0.21.0):** in someone else's battle the old dot-grid panel never comes back. On your brother's turn you click the real map (a tile twice to move, a skill then a target). If the game's turn bar can't be moved to your brother, a small action strip above the skill bar holds his skills, Wait and End turn. The turn bar now waits for each step to finish instead of skipping past your brother. The host's game now sends an update whenever a unit moves or is hurt (at most twice a second), not only at turn changes, units that don't arrive by their slide are put in place, and the joiner's log lists any units still out of place.
- **Real battlefield for joiners (v0.20.0):** when you join someone else's battle, your game now builds a copy of their battle in a normal tactical scene instead of the dot-grid panel. The map is generated from the same seed and terrain as the host's, every unit is copied (brothers whole, other fighters as script, gear and looks), and each update from the host moves units, sets hitpoints, armor, AP and morale, removes the dead and adds the host's combat log lines. Nothing acts on its own in the copy. On your brothers' turns the turn bar is moved to them, so you use the normal skill bar and click the map; moves (click a tile twice) and skills become orders that the host's game carries out. If the copy can't follow (the turn bar can't reach your brother, for example), the old battle panel opens for your turns. Mod Options > "Real battlefield in shared battles" turns it off. Both players need v0.18.0 (protocol 3). Also: the "Co-op battle" reopen button is readable, and the battle panel closes when you leave the campaign.
- **One campaign, one clock (v0.18.0):** joining puts you in the host's campaign: same map, the host's combat and economic difficulty, and the host's day and hour from the moment your company arrives (the trip there costs no food and spoils nothing). After that everyone shares the host's clock. When the host pauses (spacebar or the pause button), the clock stops and every joiner's world pauses too until the host unpauses (v0.24.0); joiners can't unpause meanwhile. A joiner's own pause, a town, the inventory or a contract screen only stop their own company while the day keeps counting, and after a battle you come back to the shared day. The host's speed buttons set the speed for everyone; while any player is in a battle (or has brothers away in someone else's), time is held at normal speed for everyone on the map, so nobody fast-forwards days while a friend fights. All co-op players need v0.18.0 (protocol 3).
- **Contract panel for helpers (v0.22.0):** when you help with someone's contract, the contract panel in the top right of your world map shows their contract, the same as theirs: the title, the objectives (where to go, what to destroy or escort) and the payment lines, kept up to date as the contract moves on, plus who took it and how many ways payments are split. When an objective changes you also get a note. Clicking the panel offers to stop helping (it never cancels the owner's contract). A contract of your own always takes the panel; the shared one comes back when yours ends. All co-op players need v0.22.0 (protocol 5).
- **Caravan escorts (v0.19.0):** in a caravan escort contract the game ties your company to the caravan and moves you with it. Everyone helping with that contract is now moved along too: their company rides next to yours until the escort is over (the caravan reaches its town, is lost, or the contract ends). While travelling with the caravan a helper can't give move orders or camp and uses no provisions, the same as the contract owner. When the caravan is attacked, helpers get the usual Battle nearby prompt to join the fight. A helper who is in a town or a menu when the escort starts is picked up as soon as they leave it. Helping someone else's contract, or the owner going offline for a minute, lets go. Both games need v0.19.0 (an older game just doesn't follow).
- **Shared contracts (v0.15.0):** when you take a contract, your online clanmates get a prompt to help with it (title, employer, objectives, target). Everyone who says yes gets an even share of every payment made after that: whoever took the contract pays each online helper their share automatically. The advance paid on signing stays with whoever signed. A helper who is offline when a payment comes keeps no share of it. `Ctrl+O` asks clanmates again (for someone who came online later) and shows who is helping, or whose contract you help with. Helpers fight the contract battles alongside the owner (`Ctrl+B` joins a nearby battle). Helping someone new stops helping the old one. All co-op players need v0.15.0: a v0.14.0 game is turned away from a v0.15.0 host.
- **Paste once (v0.17.1):** in the game's UI one Ctrl+V could paste an invite code many times, so the joiner's game tried a junk address (and got errors like status 401). Text fields now take one paste per Ctrl+V, cut a repeated paste back to one copy, and the join window reads a code or address that was pasted several times.
- **One window to host (v0.17.0):** double-click `relay/start-host.bat`. It asks for a password, starts the relay and opens the free Cloudflare tunnel itself (`node relay.js --tunnel`), so friends can join over the internet with no port forward and no second window. Put `cloudflared-windows-amd64.exe` (download: https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflared/releases/latest) in the `relay` folder or on the PATH. When the tunnel is up the relay prints the invite code, and `Ctrl+I` in game shows it ("starting..." until then). If the tunnel drops, the relay restarts it (up to 5 times); its address then changes, and the game tells you that your invite code changed. Without cloudflared the relay still runs and says what is missing. `start-relay.bat` still starts the relay alone (LAN, Tailscale or your own port forward).
- **Invite codes and your hosts (v0.16.0):** the host presses `Ctrl+I` for an invite code (`BB1-...`) to send friends; the relay window prints it too. The code holds the address friends use: the relay takes it from `--address=...` (or `BBCOOP_ADDRESS`), from what the host pastes in the `Ctrl+I` window (e.g. the tunnel's https address), or finds the PC's Tailscale address by itself. **Join a world** now lists your hosts: paste a code (or a plain address) once and the host stays in the list. Each is checked live: online or offline, session name, day, players, password, round-trip time, and in red why you can't join (different co-op version, different DLCs, full). A host whose tunnel address changed only needs the new code; it updates the same entry. Hosts you join are added by themselves.
- **Host a world (v0.14.0):** Multiplayer on the main menu now asks whether to host or join. **Host a world** (with `start-relay.bat` running on this PC) asks for your name, a session name, an optional password and how many players may join (2 to 8, you included), then opens **New campaign** or **Load a save**: the host picks the world. Your game describes the session to its relay (name, company, day, players, versions, DLCs); anyone can see that on the relay's `/ping` without the password, which the coming session list uses. The relay turns away players past your limit ("session full") and games with an incompatible co-op version. A password typed in the Host window replaces the one `start-relay.bat` asked for. Both are also under Mod Options. All co-op players need v0.14.0.
- **Co-op saves (v0.14.0):** once another player has been in a world you host, that world is marked as a co-op save, and every save of it carries the mark. In the Load screen they read "Co-op save, host it from Multiplayer" under the day. Loading one through the normal Load Campaign shows: "This save is co-op specific. To play it with your friends, load it through Multiplayer > Host a world."
- **Join window (v0.14.0):** the host's address starts empty; paste the one your host sent. **Rejoin last server** connects to the last world you joined (its host and address are shown under the buttons).

- **Text chat (v0.13.0):** press `Enter` on the world map (`Ctrl+Enter` in battle, where Enter ends the turn) to open a chat line bottom left. Enter sends, Escape closes. New messages show for 20 seconds; opening the chat shows the last 30. Game hotkeys are off while typing. Chat is also written to `log.html`. Both keys can be changed under Mod Options > Keybinds.

- **Trading (v0.12.0):** `Ctrl+T` on the world map, next to another player's company (within 500), opens the trade window: pick who to send to, click items in your stash and brothers to pick them, enter crowns, and send. The other player gets an Accept / Decline window. Your offer leaves your company while it waits and comes back if they decline, don't answer within 2 minutes, go offline, or if you save the game meanwhile. Whatever doesn't fit in their stash or roster comes back to you. Brothers keep their gear, levels and looks.

- **Shared world:** a world joined through the Multiplayer button follows the host's while the host is online. The host's roaming parties appear on the joiner's map and move where they move on the host's; parties and camps the joiner's own world spawns are removed; camps, lairs and ruins the host has are added with the same defenders and loot; burned farms and mines and each town's situations are copied. Whatever a joiner destroys (a party or a camp) is destroyed in the host's world too, and survivors of a joiner's battle keep their losses. The joiner's own contract targets are left alone, so contracts work.
- **Enemies hunt joiners too (v0.11.0):** the host's enemy parties see the other players' companies (with their real strength) and chase them like they chase the host. When one catches a joiner, the joiner's game starts that fight against its copy of the party, as if its own world had attacked; the host's party waits 20 seconds meanwhile. Whether a party is hostile follows the host's relations with its faction. When the host is offline the joiner's world runs on its own.
- **Typing in co-op windows:** Enter confirms, Escape closes, and game hotkeys are off while a text field has focus.

- **Session password:** `start-relay.bat` asks for a password (Enter for none), or run `node relay.js --password=...`. Players enter it in the Multiplayer window (or Mod Options). Recommended whenever the relay is reachable from the internet (tunnel or port forward).

- **Loot for joiners: what you kill, you get.** When the host wins, each enemy's gear goes to the player whose brother killed it, gear from a joiner's own fallen brothers goes back to them, and loot no single enemy carried (crowns, the party's stash, ammo, armor parts) is shared by number of kills. A joiner's share lands in their stash; crowns and supplies go straight to their stock. Only allies who are still connected get a share; players who joined against the host get none.

- **Safe sessions:** games send a heartbeat every 5 seconds, also during battles. If a player goes quiet for 20 seconds mid-battle, the host controls their brothers until they are back. If the host goes quiet for 60 seconds (crash, quit), or the battle ends without results, the joiner's brothers come home unharmed. While your brothers are away the world stays paused and your own fights are blocked. If the relay restarts, games notice and catch up.

- **Same fight, same battle:** attacking (or being attacked) within 400 units of another player's ongoing battle asks whether to join it instead of starting your own. If your side wins, the parties you had attacked are beaten in your world too. A battle nearby (within the join range) also pops up a join prompt.
- **Clans and sides:** `Ctrl+K` opens the clan window: create a clan or join another player's. Clanmates always join on the same side; anyone else chooses to join on the host's side or against the host. Brothers joining against the host fight for the enemy and are played by their owner.
- **DLC check:** joining a world is refused when the two games have different DLCs, because the maps would differ.

- **Multiplayer button (main menu):** enter the host's relay address and your name, press Connect. The host's game (connected and on the world map) answers with its map seed; the new campaign screen opens with that seed filled in. Pick your origin and settings, and when the campaign starts your company is placed next to the host's.

- **Play your own brothers in someone else's battle:** the host's game runs the battle. When one of your brothers is up, the host's game waits and a battle panel opens on your screen showing the battlefield (yours green, allies blue, enemies red). Click a tile to move, or pick a skill and then a highlighted tile; Wait and End turn work as usual. Between your turns the panel shows whose turn it is. If no order arrives for 90 seconds, the host gets control of that brother for the turn.

- **Join a battle as reinforcements:** when another player's battle starts and your company is within the join range (Mod Options, default 600), you get a notification. Press `Ctrl+B` to send your frontline brothers (up to 12). They arrive on free tiles along the map edge nearest the host's company and fight on the host's side. When the battle ends, each brother comes back to your roster with their new wounds, XP and gear, or is buried with an obituary entry if they fell.

- **Ghost companies:** while you are on the world map, your company's position is sent about once a second. The other player's game shows your company there as a friendly party that follows you smoothly. It never fights, is never saved, and disappears 20 seconds after you go quiet (closing the game, going to the main menu).
- For positions to line up, both players must start a **new campaign with the same map seed** (copy the seed from the new-campaign screen). You get a warning if the seeds differ. Campaigns started before installing v0.2.0 don't know their seed, so no check happens there.

- On the main menu, pings the relay and shows an on-screen notification (top right) saying whether it is reachable.
- Joins a session and shows which other players are connected.
- Tests whether the game's browser supports WebSockets (result in `log.html`).
- `Ctrl+H` (world map or battle) sends a "hello" notification to the other player (you get a "Hello sent" confirmation).
- `Ctrl+J` re-runs the connection test (use it after changing settings).

## Requirements

- Battle Brothers (vanilla, with or without DLCs)
- [Modern Hooks](https://github.com/MSUTeam/Modern-Hooks) and [MSU](https://github.com/MSUTeam/MSU) (MSU requires Modern Hooks)
- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) 16 or newer, only on the PC that runs the relay

## Install

1. Build the mod zip: `python build.py` (writes `dist/mod_bb_coop.zip`), or download it from the release/PR.
2. Copy `mod_bb_coop.zip` into `Battle Brothers/data/` next to the MSU and Modern Hooks zips.
3. On one PC, start the relay: double-click `relay/start-host.bat` on Windows (relay plus free tunnel, see above) or `relay/start-relay.bat` (relay only), or run `node relay/relay.js` (default port 7777). Keep its window open while playing.

## Testing with two players

1. Player 1 runs the relay. Player 2 must be able to reach that PC on port 7777 (same network, a port forward, or a VPN like Tailscale/ZeroTier/Hamachi).
2. In game, open **Mod Options > Battle Brothers Co-op**:
   - **Relay URL:** player 1 keeps `http://127.0.0.1:7777`; player 2 uses `http://<player 1's IP>:7777`.
   - **Player name:** each player picks a different name.
3. Press `Ctrl+J` (or restart) to reconnect with the new settings.
4. Start campaigns with the same map seed, then press `Ctrl+H`. The other player should get a notification, and each of you should see the other's company on the map.

**Testing alone:** for the Multiplayer button, play any campaign for a few seconds while the bot relay runs (the bot remembers that world in `relay/bot-state.json`), quit to the main menu and use Multiplayer: the bot answers as host. The bot also keeps the last whole world your game sent while it was running (v0.10.0), so the joined campaign follows that world: its parties stand still, and what you destroy is taken out of the bot's copy (the relay window says so). Hostile parties within 500 units walk towards you and attack when they reach you. For trading, the bot accepts whatever you offer (except a few crowns on their own, which it declines) and sends you 100 crowns when you say hello (`Ctrl+H`). It also answers in chat. Join "Bot Clan" and the bot shares a test contract with you: say Help and it pays you 150 crowns ten seconds later, then escorts a pretend caravan east for 15 seconds so your company is moved along with it (v0.19.0); share your own contract and it helps, and when you take an escort it rides next to you; share your own contract and it helps (the relay window shows its share). Start the relay with `relay/start-relay-with-bot.bat` (or `node relay/relay.js --bot`). It adds a fake player called "Bot" whose company circles yours on the world map and answers your hellos. When you start a battle, Bot joins 3 seconds later as reinforcements, with a company of its own (one origin, picked at random and remembered; choose one with `--origin=deserters` etc.), and plays them itself. It is in the clan "Bot Clan": join it with `Ctrl+K` and it fights on your side, otherwise it joins against you. It attacks enemies in reach, otherwise steps towards the nearest enemy and ends its turn.

If something goes wrong, send the `[BBCoop]` lines from `Documents/Battle Brothers/log.html`.

## Layout

- `mod/`: the mod, zipped as-is into `mod_bb_coop.zip`
  - `scripts/!mods_preload/mod_bb_coop.nut`: registers with Modern Hooks and MSU
  - `bb_coop/load.nut`: settings, keybinds, connection setup
  - `bb_coop/ghosts.nut`: ghost companies on the world map, map seed tracking
  - `bb_coop/roster.nut`: brothers to network data and back (MSU serialization emulator)
  - `bb_coop/battle.nut`: shared battles: invites, reinforcements at the map edge, results
  - `bb_coop/remote.nut`: remote turns: battle views for the other player, carrying out their orders
  - `scripts/mods/bb_coop/bb_coop_connection.nut`: Squirrel side of the bridge
  - `ui/mods/bb_coop/bb_coop_connection.js`: network layer (HTTP to the relay)
- `relay/relay.js`: relay server, no dependencies (`--bot` adds a fake player)
- `tests/js_client_harness.js`: runs two simulated clients' JS against the relay: `node tests/js_client_harness.js`
