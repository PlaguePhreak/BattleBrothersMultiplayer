# Battle Brothers Multiplayer (BB Co-op)

A mod that aims to let two players share one Battle Brothers world map, each running their own company, and fight together as allies.

Battle Brothers has no networking, but the game's UI browser can make HTTP requests. This mod uses that to talk to a small **relay server** that both players connect to.

## What it does

- **Joining after a stalled connection (v0.45.0):** the game sends its messages one at a time, and a request the relay never answered left the queue stuck for good: the joiner's "ask the host for its world" never left, and the join window ended in "No host answered" although the relay listed the host online. A send now gives up after 12 seconds, the queue is cleared whenever a connection starts or stops, and the join request goes straight to the relay.
- **Gear in shared battles (v0.45.0):** the inventory screen stays closed for another player's brother and in a copied battle, with a notice why: a swap there only showed on the local screen (the host swapped a guest's weapons and the guest never saw it).
- **New settlements (v0.44.0, protocol 10):** a prosperous town can send settlers out to found a small village 4 to 7 tiles away, joined to it by a new road. Prosperous means a northern town or village (no castles, no southern cities) with a noble owner, not burned, none of its farms, mines or camps burned, no bad situation (raided, sickness, ambushed trade routes, short on food and the like) and at least one good one (well supplied, good harvest, seasonal fair, safe roads and the like); cities count without a good situation. Nothing happens before day 20; after that at most one new village every 10 days in the whole world (a 25% chance each new day once the wait is over), each town founds at most 2 villages and waits 50 days between them, a new village waits 50 days before it can found one, and a world gets at most 10. The village type follows the land (vanilla's small village list and terrain rules), it belongs to the founder's noble house and has its own council and contracts. Only the host's world founds; joiners get the same village on the same tile with the same name and road from the host's world updates. Founded villages and their roads are kept in the save (and redrawn on load). Host option: Mod Options > New settlements.
- **Reload orders on the host (v0.43.4):** a guest's Reload order answered "Co-op: Unknown skill." when the host's copy of that brother had no reload skill at that moment. The host now rebuilds the skill from the weapon's loaded state before giving up, and logs the brother's skills when an order still names an unknown one.
- **Reload in copied battles (v0.43.3):** vanilla only keeps the Reload skill while a crossbow or handgonne is empty (the shot adds it, the reload removes it). The copy set the weapon to empty but never added the skill, so the Reload button was missing until the brother re-equipped the weapon. The copy now adds and removes it like vanilla does.
- **Different brothers for the same origin (v0.43.2):** the v0.26 reroll came after vanilla had already made the starting brothers (they are rolled in the origin's `onSpawnAssets`, not `onSpawnPlayer`), so a joiner who picked the host's origin still got the host's exact men. The dice are now re-seeded with the map seed plus the player's name before the brothers are made.
- **Escort and sync fixes (v0.43.1, from Aaron's and his friend's logs):** a helper riding with a friend's caravan who got attacked was stuck on the battle loading screen: the mod kept moving the company onto the caravan while the combat dialog was open, so by the time the battle loaded nobody was left to fight at that spot, and vanilla hides the map but never the loading screen in that case. The company no longer moves while a dialog, menu or loading screen is up or the game is paused, and an empty battle start now returns to the map with a notice. Ctrl+clicking (force attack) another player's company crashed the game (vanilla looked up the player faction, which does not exist): those companies can no longer be attacked by the player. Camps the joiner's world replaces with the host's were removed and re-added every 5 seconds (`die()` left them alive); they fade out instead, camps we spawned are matched even if placed on a neighbouring tile, and camps waiting for a tile to clear are counted separately in the log.
- **Wiped-out companies (v0.43.0, protocol 9):** a joiner whose brothers all die (in a shared battle or their own) is asked to start a new company in the host's world (the origin's starting brothers, starting crowns and supplies, an empty stash; it replaces the old one in the host's save) or to leave the session (vanilla's defeat screen; the host drops their company, new message `company_drop`, so they start fresh when they join again). The host can reset any player's company from Mod Options (type the name, press the button): the stored company is removed and that player's game goes back to the main menu (new message `company_reset`). Fixes from a friend's log: a joiner who watched the real battlefield got their result but it was never applied (the copy still counted as open when the world took the result back, so the dead came home alive); the result now applies as the copy closes, with a fallback on the next world frame. The default origin's intro event reads the first three brothers and failed for a restored two-man company; vanilla then tried to show it every frame, playing the event sound each time (the "audio issue"): origin intro events are skipped in joined worlds and an event whose screen fails is dropped. A `contract_track` message during campaign setup no longer logs an error.
- **Review follow-ups (v0.42.1, Aaron):** a player who is in their own battle (or away in someone else's) when a friend's battle starts is asked to join it as soon as they are back on the world map, if it is still going and still in range. A joiner whose host has no stored company for them is told so when they start fresh. After a copy of someone else's battle, vanilla no longer puts a battlefield marker where the joiner's company stands or stuns the parties around it (checked against the decompiled world_state.onCombatFinished). Same protocol (8).
- **Code-review fixes (v0.42.0, protocol 8):** a joiner watching the real battlefield got "No results arrived" and unharmed brothers after every shared battle, because the host's `battle_over` reached them while the result was being kept for the world map; results now wait up to 8 seconds and are never dropped while one is kept. Messages leave the game one after the other, so two sent back to back can't swap places on the way. The relay no longer treats requests through the cloudflared tunnel as coming from the host PC (`/host`, `/address`, `/invite` were open to everyone with the invite code). A joiner's company copies carry a revision; the host refuses an older copy (loading an old local save of a joined world no longer rolls the company back in the host's save, the joiner is told, new message `company_refused`). A result or trade that fails to unpack no longer leaves a half-made brother in the roster. Players queued to join at the end of a round get a result when the battle ends first. The relay keeps one-off messages longer than position and view updates and tells a client that missed some. Views between turns leave the terrain out. Ctrl+J only pings the relay instead of re-joining. The relay forgets a session card the host's game stopped sending.
- **No freeze on deaths and retreats (v0.41.0):** v0.39.0 only took a dead brother off the host's turn bar when he was still due this round, so he could come back next round and freeze the battle. Dead and retreated brothers are now always taken off, and the host's turn bar never hands the turn to a dead or departed unit. Same protocol (7).
- **Turn bar follows every turn (v0.40.0, Aaron):** the joiner's turn bar moves on whenever the host's battle moves to the next unit, not only for the joiner's own brothers. For other players' units it never moves past the end of the round the host is in, so it can't run ahead of the host. Same protocol (7).
- **No freeze on a friend's death (v0.39.0):** when a friend's brother died in the host's battle, he could stay in the host's turn bar; when his turn came the host's game waited forever. Dead units are now taken off the turn bar, and a dead unit that still gets a turn is skipped. Same protocol (7).
- **Turn notice and reloading (v0.38.0):** in a shared battle the top right says "It's your turn" on the screen of the player whose brother acts (the host sees "Touko's turn" while a friend's brother acts). Crossbows and other weapons that need reloading now offer Reload in a joiner's copy (the loaded state comes from the host).
- **No action strip (v0.38.0):** the joiner's extra bar with skills, Wait and End turn is gone; the game's own skill bar, End turn button and the map are the controls. If the copy's turn bar can't reach your brother, the game's End turn and Wait still work for him. Same protocol (7).
- **Late joiners (v0.37.0, Aaron's rule):** players who join within the 20-second window start next to their side; anyone who joins after that watches the battle and their brothers arrive at the map edge when the current round ends, so they act from the next round. Same protocol (7).
- **Shared battles work again (v0.36.0):** since v0.28.0 the battle-copy script (mirror.nut) failed to load because of a reserved word, so joiners never got a copy of the battle, and the host's join window stalled. Fixed; everything listed for v0.28.0 to v0.33.0 now actually runs. Same protocol as v0.35.0.
- **Every company in the host's save (v0.35.0):** joiners keep no saves, so their game now sends the host a full copy of their company every 90 seconds and right after the host saves. The host keeps the newest copy of each player (by player name) and writes all of them into every save it makes (manual, quick and autosave, through MSU flags). A copy holds every brother exactly as a save file holds them (stats, perks, traits, injuries, gear, XP, formation) plus their looks, the whole stash (food included), crowns, tools, medicine, ammo, renown and moral reputation, company name, banner, look and origin, followers, and the spot on the map. When a player joins that world again, the host sends their copy back: they click through the new campaign screen as usual and their stored company replaces the starter one where they left off. A player with nothing stored starts fresh, and that company is what gets kept from then on. If restoring goes wrong, the joiner's game never overwrites the good copy. Not carried yet: an accepted contract. All co-op players need v0.35.0 (protocol 7).
- **Attack animations for joiners (v0.33.0):** a joiner's copy of the battle now replays every skill the host's game uses: the skill's sound, arrows and other shots flying for ranged skills, the attacker lunging and the target flinching, one after another in order. Hitpoints and deaths still come from the host. Same protocol (6).
- **Joining uses the host's settings (v0.32.0):** a joiner picks only their origin, company name and banner. Difficulty, economic difficulty, ironman, unexplored map, late game crisis and permanent destruction are the host's, shown greyed out on the new campaign screen and forced by the game whatever is sent. Starting funds aren't kept in a save, so every joiner starts on Medium. Cancelling the screen cancels the join.
- **Joiner input fix (v0.31.1):** in a copy of someone else's battle, a unit the copy couldn't put in place (shown as "Copy differs from the host" in the log) kept the map locked for the whole turn, so the joiner couldn't do anything. Now only your own acting brother's move holds the input. Same protocol (6).
- **Join window (v0.31.0):** when a battle starts and other players are online, it waits up to 20 seconds before round 1. Nearby players get the join prompt with the countdown; whoever joins in time has their brothers placed right next to their side (not at the map edge) and plays them from round 1. The battle starts as soon as everyone has joined or said no. Players out of range or busy say no by themselves. Joining later still works and brings brothers in at the edge as before. All co-op players need v0.31.0 (protocol 6).
- **Banners on the map (v0.29.0):** other players' companies carry their own banner and keep their "X's company" name, like a player's company. A clanmate's company carries your banner.
- **Turn marker (v0.28.0):** in a shared battle, the brother whose turn it is (anyone's) has the game's red arrow hopping over his head, on the host's screen and in every joiner's copy, so everyone can see who is acting. Also fixed: when one of the host's brothers died, the joiner's copy kept him in its turn bar, then locked up (and could crash when a skill was selected); the copy now takes dead units off its turn bar.

The host's game now sends an update whenever a unit moves or is hurt (at most twice a second), not only at turn changes, units that don't arrive by their slide are put in place, and the joiner's log lists any units still out of place.

The map is generated from the same seed and terrain as the host's, every unit is copied (brothers whole, other fighters as script, gear and looks), and each update from the host moves units, sets hitpoints, armor, AP and morale, removes the dead and adds the host's combat log lines. Nothing acts on its own in the copy. On your brothers' turns the turn bar is moved to them, so you use the normal skill bar and click the map; moves (click a tile twice) and skills become orders that the host's game carries out. If the copy can't follow (the turn bar can't reach your brother, for example), the old battle panel opens for your turns. Mod Options > "Real battlefield in shared battles" turns it off. - **Shared world:** a world joined through the Multiplayer button follows the host's while the host is online. The host's roaming parties appear on the joiner's map and move where they move on the host's; parties and camps the joiner's own world spawns are removed; camps, lairs and ruins the host has are added with the same defenders and loot; burned farms and mines and each town's situations are copied. Whatever a joiner destroys (a party or a camp) is destroyed in the host's world too, and survivors of a joiner's battle keep their losses. The joiner's own contract targets are left alone, so contracts work.
- **Enemies hunt joiners too (v0.11.0):** the host's enemy parties see the other players' companies (with their real strength) and chase them like they chase the host. Whether a party is hostile follows the host's relations with its faction. When the host is offline the joiner's world runs on its own.
- **Typing in co-op windows:** Enter confirms, Escape closes, and game hotkeys are off while a text field has focus.

- **Session password:** `start-relay.bat` asks for a password (Enter for none), or run `node relay.js --password=...`. Players enter it in the Multiplayer window (or Mod Options). Recommended whenever the relay is reachable from the internet (tunnel or port forward).

- **Loot for joiners: what you kill, you get.** When the host wins, each enemy's gear goes to the player whose brother killed it, gear from a joiner's own fallen brothers goes back to them, and loot no single enemy carried (crowns, the party's stash, ammo, armor parts) is shared by number of kills. A joiner's share lands in their stash; crowns and supplies go straight to their stock. Only allies who are still connected get a share; players who joined against the host get none.

- **Safe sessions:** games send a heartbeat every 5 seconds, also during battles. If a player goes quiet for 20 seconds mid-battle, the host controls their brothers until they are back. If the host goes quiet for 60 seconds (crash, quit), or the battle ends without results, the joiner's brothers come home unharmed. While your brothers are away the world stays paused and your own fights are blocked. If the relay restarts, games notice and catch up.

- **Same fight, same battle:** attacking (or being attacked) within 400 units of another player's ongoing battle asks whether to join it instead of starting your own. If your side wins, the parties you had attacked are beaten in your world too. A battle nearby (within the join range) also pops up a join prompt.
- **Clans and sides:** `Ctrl+K` opens the clan window: create a clan or join another player's. Clanmates always join on the same side; anyone else chooses to join on the host's side or against the host. Brothers joining against the host fight for the enemy and are played by their owner.
- **DLC check:** joining a world is refused when the two games have different DLCs, because the maps would differ.

- **Multiplayer button (main menu):** enter the host's relay address and your name, press Connect. The host's game (connected and on the world map) answers with its map seed; the new campaign screen opens with that seed filled in. Pick your origin and settings, and when the campaign starts your company is placed next to the host's.

- **Play your own brothers in someone else's battle:** the host's game runs the battle. When one of your brothers is up, the host's game waits and a battle panel opens on your screen showing the battlefield (yours green, allies blue, enemies red). Click a tile to move, or pick a skill and then a highlighted tile; Wait and End turn work as usual. Between your turns the panel shows whose turn it is. If no order arrives for 90 seconds, the host gets control of that brother for the turn.

- **Join a battle as reinforcements:** when another player's battle starts and your company is within the join range (Mod Options, default 600), you get a notification. Press `Ctrl+B` to send your frontline brothers (up to 12). They arrive on free tiles along the map edge nearest the host's company and fight on the host's side. When the battle ends, each brother comes back to your roster with their new wounds, XP and gear, or is buried with an obituary entry if they fell.

- **Ghost companies:** while you are on the world map, your company's position is sent about once a second. The other player's game shows your company there as a friendly party that follows you smoothly. It never fights, is never saved, and disappears 20 seconds after you go quiet (closing the game, going to the main menu).
- For positions to line up, both players must start a **new campaign with the same map seed** (copy the seed from the new-campaign screen). You get a warning if the seeds differ. Campaigns started before installing v0.2.0 don't know their seed, so no check happens there.

- On the main menu, pings the relay and shows an on-screen notification (top right) saying whether it is reachable.
- Joins a session and shows which other players are connected.
- Tests whether the game's browser supports WebSockets (result in `log.html`).
- `Ctrl+H` (world map or battle) sends a "hello" notification to the other player (you get a "Hello sent" confirmation).
- `Ctrl+J` re-runs the connection test (use it after changing settings).

## Requirements

- Battle Brothers (vanilla, with or without DLCs)
- [Modern Hooks](https://github.com/MSUTeam/Modern-Hooks) and [MSU](https://github.com/MSUTeam/MSU) (MSU requires Modern Hooks)
- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) 16 or newer, only on the PC that runs the relay

## Install

1. Build the mod zip: `python build.py` (writes `dist/mod_bb_coop.zip`), or download it from the release/PR.
2. Copy `mod_bb_coop.zip` into `Battle Brothers/data/` next to the MSU and Modern Hooks zips.
3. On one PC, start the relay: double-click `relay/start-host.bat` on Windows (relay plus free tunnel, see above) or `relay/start-relay.bat` (relay only), or run `node relay/relay.js` (default port 7777). Keep its window open while playing.

## Testing with two players

1. Player 1 runs the relay. Player 2 must be able to reach that PC on port 7777 (same network, a port forward, or a VPN like Tailscale/ZeroTier/Hamachi).
2. In game, open **Mod Options > Battle Brothers Co-op**:
   - **Relay URL:** player 1 keeps `http://127.0.0.1:7777`; player 2 uses `http://<player 1's IP>:7777`.
   - **Player name:** each player picks a different name.

## Layout

- `mod/`: the mod, zipped as-is into `mod_bb_coop.zip`
  - `scripts/!mods_preload/mod_bb_coop.nut`: registers with Modern Hooks and MSU
  - `bb_coop/load.nut`: settings, keybinds, connection setup
  - `bb_coop/ghosts.nut`: ghost companies on the world map, map seed tracking
  - `bb_coop/roster.nut`: brothers to network data and back (MSU serialization emulator)
  - `bb_coop/battle.nut`: shared battles: invites, reinforcements at the map edge, results
  - `bb_coop/remote.nut`: remote turns: battle views for the other player, carrying out their orders
  - `scripts/mods/bb_coop/bb_coop_connection.nut`: Squirrel side of the bridge
  - `ui/mods/bb_coop/bb_coop_connection.js`: network layer (HTTP to the relay)
- `relay/relay.js`: relay server, no dependencies (`--bot` adds a fake player)
- `tests/js_client_harness.js`: runs two simulated clients' JS against the relay: `node tests/js_client_harness.js`
